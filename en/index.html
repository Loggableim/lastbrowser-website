What Lastbrowser does

A browser that doesn't just load pages — it works with you. The sidebar brings Sidekick directly into the browser window for research, writing, tasks, and projects.

Chat in the Sidebar

Ask questions, understand content, draft text — right next to the page, no tab switching.

Tasks at a Glance

Create tasks from context, prioritize them, and keep them open until done.

Launch Skills Instantly

Built-in workflows for research, writing, analysis, and more — ready to go.

Spaces for Projects

Each project gets its own space with cleanly separated context and history.

Keep Context

Important info stays preserved and is available on the next step.

Windows Browser

Available as Setup or Portable, quick to start, built for daily use.

How it works

Three steps: install, use Sidekick, keep projects clean in Spaces.

01

Install

Download Setup or Portable, launch, open the browser. No detours — straight to productive.

02

Use Sidekick

The sidebar stays open while you browse. Chat, ask, summarize, act.

03

Work with Spaces

Keep projects separate so research, tasks, and history don't get mixed up.
