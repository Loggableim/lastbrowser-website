Release cycle

Next beta: 0.1.57 today, 16 October 2026, 20:00 Vienna time. Then one new beta each day: 17 October is 0.1.58, 18 October is 0.1.59.

Next release window: Thursday, 22 October 2026, 16:20. The daily beta before it is 0.1.62. The release jumps to the next ten, so 0.1.70. Daily betas then continue: 23 October is 0.1.71, 24 October is 0.1.72. Then every two weeks: 5 November 2026 jumps to 0.1.90, 19 November 2026 to 0.1.110. The beta going into the release is announced one or two days before. No stable tag before that.

The app can subscribe to the Stable or Beta update channel. Beta builds are not signed on every upload.

Beta 0.1.57 is in preparation. Beta builds are unsigned, there is no stable tag.
