For Agencies, SMMs & Consultants

Every client account in parallel in one window

Forget time-consuming profile windows or constant logging in and out. In LastBrowser every Space owns an isolated partition for cookies, caches and logins.

Download LastBrowser for Windows View Spaces architecture
🪐

True Session Partitioning

Every Space uses its own partition: persist:profile:<spaceId>. Logins on Google, AWS, GitHub or LinkedIn stay strictly separated.

🎯

No Profile-Window Chaos

Switch between clients, projects or mandates in a flash in the vertical sidebar, without keeping a dozen separate desktop windows open.

🔒

Zero Cookie Bleed

Ruled out: accidentally posting to the wrong client account. Session data cannot leak into other Spaces by design.

🎨

Color Coding & Icons

Each Space can be assigned its own name, accent color and icon, so you always recognize instantly which client context you are working in.

⚡

Memory Discarding

Inactive Spaces automatically free their RAM. Even with 10 active client Spaces your PC stays fluid and responsive.

🛡️

Client Privacy

All client credentials stay encrypted on your local drive under %APPDATA%\Lastbrowser and are never tracked.
